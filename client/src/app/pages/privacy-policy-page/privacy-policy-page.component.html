In short

  • You sign in with a link sent as a foodsharing message. We never see your foodsharing password.
  • We only store what is needed to organise EinAbs: name, Foodsharing ID, phone number, bookings and notes.
  • Only the EinAb guides of your appointment and the ambassadors of your district see these data.
  • No tracking, no advertising cookies, no analytics.
  • Data are deleted at the latest 24 months after your last booking or sign-in. You can delete your account yourself at any time.

1. Who is responsible

Eric Zürcher, info@it4user.ch.

Einab.ch is a volunteer tool that foodsharing districts in Switzerland use to organise introductory pickups (EinAbs). It is not run by foodsharing e.V. or foodsharing.network. Their own privacy policy applies to their platform.

3. Sign-in

You enter your Foodsharing ID and receive a sign-in link as a foodsharing.network message. We never see or store your foodsharing password. The link is valid for 30 minutes, the sign-in afterwards for up to 365 days or until you sign out. Sign-in links and sessions are stored only as a check value (hash).

4. Who sees your data

  • The EinAb guides of your appointment: name, Foodsharing ID, phone number, your bookings in this district, number of EinAbs, notes, last sign-in.
  • The ambassadors of your district: the same data, to manage EinAbs and accounts.
  • You: after booking, the guide's name and phone number, the meeting point and private info about the appointment.
  • Public (without sign-in): only the guide's name, the date and the general place of an appointment. Participant names are never public.
  • Surveys: only the person who created the survey.

Everyone with access is a foodsharing member with a role in the district and bound by foodsharing's confidentiality rules. We never sell data or pass it on for advertising.

5. Services we use

  • foodsharing.network (Germany): sign-in messages, notifications, lookup of name, phone number and verification.
  • Proton AG (Switzerland): sending e-mails.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC, USA), only in the EinAb app with push notifications turned on: delivers push notifications to your phone. It receives the push token and the title and text of the notification. On iPhones, Google hands the notification to the Apple Push Notification service (Apple Inc., USA). Google LLC is certified under the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
  • Hosting provider in Germany: server and database.

District ambassadors can use extra automations (connections to foodsharing.network and Telegram). These are enabled only for single authorised people and do not involve your account as a participant.

Germany and the EU have adequate data protection under Swiss law. For the USA, transfers to companies certified under the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework are considered adequate.

The EinAb app (Google Play, App Store) shows this website; it collects no other data than the website, apart from the push token above. Push notifications can be turned off in your profile or in your phone's settings at any time.

6. Legal basis

We follow the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the GDPR. We process your data to provide the EinAb service you want to use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), in the district's legitimate interest in organised onboarding of new foodsavers (Art. 6(1)(f)), and for notifications and surveys with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

8. Your rights

At any time you can:

  • ask what data we hold about you and get a copy,
  • have wrong data corrected (you can change your phone number in your profile yourself),
  • delete your account and your data yourself: in your profile under "Delete account", or without signing in at einab.ch/konto-loeschen (confirmed with a link sent as foodsharing message),
  • object to processing and withdraw consent (you can unsubscribe from notifications with the link in every message).

Write to info@it4user.ch. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC, www.edoeb.admin.ch) or, if you live in the EU, to the data protection authority of your country.

9. Security

The connection is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in links and sessions are stored only as hashes, credentials for foodsharing connections encrypted. Only people with the matching role can access data. If a data breach with high risk still happens, we inform those affected and the FDPIC.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we show you the new version at your next sign-in and ask you to confirm it.
